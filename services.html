We transmit prescriptions directly into the SureScripts nationwide pharmacy network. Your medication will be waiting at your chosen pharmacy counter or available for home delivery.

Important Prescription Policy: Horizon Virtual Care adheres to all federal and state telehealth prescribing laws. We DO NOT prescribe DEA Schedule II–V controlled substances (e.g., Adderall, Xanax, Ambien, Oxycodone, Testosterone) via telehealth.

When to Use Telehealth vs. Emergency Room

Helping you make the safest decision for your healthcare needs.

Ideal for Horizon Virtual Care
  • • Sinus infections, cold, flu, cough, and sore throat
  • • Urinary tract infections and painful urination
  • • Pink eye, conjunctivitis, and minor allergies
  • • Rash, hives, insect bites, minor burns, and eczema
  • • Asthma inhaler and routine maintenance drug refills
  • • Therapy for anxiety, depression, and stress management
Go to Emergency Room / Call 911
  • • Severe chest pain or sudden shortness of breath
  • • Symptoms of stroke (facial drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech)
  • • Heavy uncontrolled bleeding or deep lacerations
  • • Head trauma, loss of consciousness, or severe convulsions
  • • Severe acute poisoning or drug overdose
  • • Compound fractures or severe trauma
